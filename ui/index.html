Volume

Windows doesn't show an audio output for the headset.

Default output

?

Windows' default playback device. Pick another one to switch to it.

THX sound enhancements

?
  • Bass Boost
  • Sound Normalization
  • Voice Clarity
  • THX Spatial Audio

THX preset: (follows the headset's EQ preset)

Each level is sent when you let go of the slider, and kept while its switch is off.

The THX service (VSSrv) doesn't answer: the settings are shown but can't be changed.

THX Spatial Audio isn't installed on the headset's output. Synapse installs it, and it keeps working after Synapse is closed or removed. It can also be installed on its own.

Without THX, Windows has its own enhancements (Bass Boost, Loudness Equalization): Sound settings › the headset › Properties › Enhancements.

If THX makes no difference: in Sound settings, the headset needs audio enhancements on and Windows Sonic off.

Do Not Disturb

?

Blocks incoming phone calls over Bluetooth while you use the 2.4 GHz dongle.

Only matters when the headset is on the dongle and paired with your phone at the same time.

Mic monitoring

?

Hear your own voice in the headset while you talk.

Microphone enhancements (THX)

?
  • Volume normalization
  • Vocal clarity
  • Ambient noise reduction
  • Voice gate

They change what apps record or stream, not what you hear in mic monitoring. The voice gate cuts out anything quieter than its threshold.

THX isn't available right now: the settings are saved in the profile and applied once it is.
